I've spent the last few months building something for the tradies in my life.

A tradie on the tools can't answer the phone. The caller doesn't leave a voicemail; they ring the next number on Google. That missed call was a job.

Tradie Text catches the missed call and texts the caller back in seconds. An AI conversation works out what the job is, where it is, and how urgent, then puts a scored lead on the tradie's dashboard. Emergencies get detected and escalated instead of chatting with a bot.

I designed, built, and hardened it solo: Next.js, Supabase, Twilio, Stripe, 276 tests in CI, and a proper threat model, because small businesses deserve real engineering too.

It's live and piloting with the first trades businesses now. Public launch soon.

If you know a tradie who hates their voicemail, I want to hear from them.
